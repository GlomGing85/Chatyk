# Bundled design assets

- **Material Symbols Rounded** — Google, from
  https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/symbols/android .
  Vector assets in `app/src/main/res/drawable/symbol_*.xml`, Apache License 2.0
  (see `MATERIAL_SYMBOLS_LICENSE.txt`). Tint is provided by the Compose color scheme.
- **Roboto** (Regular, Medium, Bold) — Google, from
  https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-2/tree/main/src/hinted .
  Fonts in `app/src/main/res/font`, Apache License 2.0 (see `ROBOTO_LICENSE.txt`).

These assets are bundled: the app does not download fonts or icons at runtime.
